# Draw Rush 1.2.0 (versionCode 6): release notes draft

Ready to paste into Play Console (max 500 characters per language).

Draw Rush 1.2: Draw freely!
- No more rails: walk along the dotted shape and paint it your way, leave and come back anytime
- Smoother controls: a smaller joystick, a camera that shows the whole fight, speed that builds as you run
- Power-ups on the field: Shield, Freeze, Speed and Magnet
- Upgrade shop, a Bump Pet and a Brush Buddy to help you out
- New enemies: Sprinter, Tank, Eater and Dasher
