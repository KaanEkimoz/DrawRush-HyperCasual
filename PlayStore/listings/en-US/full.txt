You are a tiny pencil on a giant desk, and the office supplies want you gone.

Draw Rush is a fast one-finger drawing game. Run along the dotted outline to draw each shape while staplers, scissors, erasers and glue bottles chase you around the page. Finish the shape before they catch you, or before they rub out your work.

HOW TO PLAY
• Move with one finger. Your pencil draws wherever it runs.
• Trace the whole outline to complete the shape.
• Stay out of the enemies' reach. Each hit makes your pencil shorter.
• Watch out for erasers: they wipe away unfinished lines.

WHY YOU WILL KEEP PLAYING
• Fill your collection: every shape you finish joins the album, and the counter shows how close you are to completing it.
• Earn up to 3 stars on every level. Clean runs pay more coins.
• Pick up power-ups: Shield, Freeze, Speed, Shockwave and a Health heart that fixes your pencil.
• Unlock 9 pencil skins, from classic crimson to golden and fountain pen styles.
• Upgrade your Speed, Health and Draw Area in the shop.
• Play on changing desks: lined, grid, dotted, pink, mint, sky blue and kraft paper.
• Face new desk monsters: a putty eraser that throws chunks of itself, a glue bottle that leaves sticky puddles, a pushpin that lunges, and a giant boss eraser.

Short levels, a hand-drawn notebook look and one simple control make it perfect for a quick break.

Grab your pencil and start the rush!
