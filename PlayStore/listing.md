## Full description (max 4000)
Draw. Rush. Escape.

DrawRush is a fast, satisfying one-finger puzzle where you draw your own path to
complete each shape — while enemies close in. Trace the line, snap the pieces
together, raise the wall, and reach the goal before you get caught.

Simple to pick up, hard to put down:
• ✏️ One-finger drawing — glide along the rails to connect every corner
• 🏃 Beat the chase — dodge or outrun the enemies hunting you mid-draw
• ⭐ 3-star levels — clean, fast runs earn more stars and more coins
• 🎨 Unlock skins — spend your coins on colorful character looks in the shop
• 🌾 Bright, chunky, low-poly world that's easy on the eyes
• 📶 Play offline — no internet needed

Every level is a quick hit of "draw it, nail it, next one." A gentle start eases
you in, then the shapes get trickier and the enemies get bolder. Chase a perfect
3-star run, stack up coins, and dress your character the way you like.

Think you can draw your way out? Start the rush.
